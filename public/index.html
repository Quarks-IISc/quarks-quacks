Quick Rules

Side Quests

  • Only 1 team can win each side quest: the first correct answer takes it, and then it's gone.
  • You get only 1 try per side quest, so make it count.
  • Side quest points add straight to your Quacks points.

Treasure Hunt

  • Starts at 10:15 AM sharp.
  • 10 problems: the first 5 come in a random order for each team, and the last 5 are in a fixed order.
  • Upload your team picture at every location for verification. If you don't, you will be disqualified.
  • If you start late, no compensation will be given.
  • Closes at 1:00 PM sharp.
